  • Plot and origin: v2 wastewater pins use the whole plot, 9144 × 18288 mm. Plot X/Y and building X/Y differ by 1944 / 2000 mm. A centre point does not establish that a tank's full envelope fits.
  • Wastewater route: trapped WCs and waste fixtures connect to sanitary branches and stacks; the buried drains feed the septic tank, and only septic effluent goes onward to the soak pit. Sewage must not bypass the tank. NBC 208 B1.3–B1.8 and UWSSP §8.4.4.
  • Pipe sizes and slope: WC branches/stacks use DN100 minimum under NBC 208 B1.4. The modeled other waste branches use DN75 as a coordination assumption. DN100 buried drain falls use the B14 1:57 benchmark; actual invert levels are part of the model schedule.
  • Sewage ventilation: keep the stack's diameter through the roof vent; do not reduce it at roof level (NBC 208 B1.7). Traps preserve a water seal. The displayed 900 mm vent extension is a modeling assumption; final termination distance from windows and accessible terrace areas is unresolved.
  • Fan exhaust is separate: toilet and kitchen extract ducts discharge to outside air; they do not connect to the sewage vent. The mechanical ducts shown are routing envelopes, not airflow/fan selections.
  • Septic capacity: for the editable assumption of eight people at 100 L/person/day, the UWSSP calculation gives 1.7064 m³ liquid storage including the two-year sludge allowance. A 2 × 1 × 1 m internal liquid envelope provides 2 m³, with freeboard and two chambers in a 2:1 length split. Both exact requested points are retained; their clashes are shown.
  • Soak pit: an internal diameter of 1.5 m and effective depth of 1.5 m would need about 113.2 L/m²/day infiltration for 800 L/day. This is the required rate, not a measured soil result. Unknown infiltration cannot be converted into a capacity claim.
  • Water and storm: keep supply water, sewage and rainwater separate. NBC 208 A2.2.5 concerns underground potable-tank separation from wastewater hazards and excludes potable tanks under parking; it is not a building-to-septic clearance rule.
  • Staircase cover and headroom: the roof cover encloses the stair room around building X2261 / Y1316 mm. Toggle it off to inspect stairs beneath. NBC 206's stair/headroom rules remain separate from structural roof thickness.
  • Electrical: the gold route is a distribution-conduit diagram only. Sanctioned load, earthing, cable sizes and protective devices require their own electrical calculations; NBC 207 explicitly covers public buildings, so it is not blindly adopted as this private home's final circuit schedule.
  • Structure and permit: the house retains the owner's envelope. NBC 205's ready-to-use size limit is a route restriction, not an instruction to shrink this design. Using the full engineered route does not establish a municipal permit or remove a physical overlap.

03 · Wastewater sizing

UWSSP v4.0 §8.4.4 calculates retention, digestion and sludge storage separately. A hydraulic size does not establish excavation safety, watertight construction, groundwater protection or municipal acceptance.

No drinking-water tank under parking. NBC 208 A2.2.5 specifies 5 m separation for underground potable tanks from wastewater hazards. This is not a building-to-septic setback. Storm drainage must stay independent.
