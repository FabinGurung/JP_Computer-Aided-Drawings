Reference mass and nominal concrete envelopes

Steel unit mass = count × (π/4) × (bar diameter / 1000)² × 7,850 kg/m³; excludes ties, anchorage, laps, bends, wastage and overhang. Gross nominal concrete envelopes exclude intersections and openings. Values are not added to the released through-plinth BOQ.
