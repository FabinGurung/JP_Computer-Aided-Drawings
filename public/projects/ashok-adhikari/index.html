Project status and next steps

Current stage: v2 architectural and MEP coordination. The working viewer is available for corrections. Structural design and the submission drawing set are the next engineering stages.

  1. Completed — source and architecture: plot/building dimensions, coordinated walls, real openings, furniture, dimensions and grid. The earlier package is retained as v1.
  2. Completed — v2 coordination: requested tank centres, fixture drains, roof vents, MEP register, staircase cover, coordinate feedback and a source-linked code library.
  3. Next — engineering resolution: resolve the marked wastewater footprint conflicts, apply measured infiltration, develop member/foundation calculations and detailed MEP schedules, and check access/finished clearances.
  4. Then — checked drawing package: synchronize plans, sections, schedules and current CAD/IFC exports for municipal submission and construction review. The earlier v1 DWG is not a new v2 drawing.

Why the Excel coordination workbook exists

The workbook makes coordinate inputs, flow assumptions, tank-volume calculations and MEP schedules easy to edit and compare. It is reusable for future coordination. Editing Excel does not move the house or publish the website. Share the edited workbook or use map pins to request a change; the change must then be applied to the project model, checked and published. Private working files stay in the project's Drive folder.

Coordinated in this edition

  • Outer wall faces now fit the exact 7200 × 14400 mm envelope.
  • Duplicate hall wall removed; all junctions share coordinates.
  • 32 door/window apertures physically cut through the 3D walls, including the 203 mm kitchen vent.
  • Stair bay 4290 × 2200 mm: width +30%; FF depth +29.4%. Two 900 mm flights, 250 mm treads, full 900 mm landings and a real upper-floor void.
  • Ground and first-floor entry doors moved onto actual walls; stairs access the porch/balcony independently.
  • Source car body retained and OEM mirrors included: 2012 mm mirror width exceeds the 2000 mm transverse strip. That parking option is not an operational fit; the 2943 mm right approach does not fix the mirror/door/turning constraints.
  • Furniture, sanitary fixtures, kitchen, floors, entry steps, glazing and guards added as explicit coordination objects.

Still outside this architectural scheme

  • Designed column sections, beams, foundations, reinforcement and structural analysis. Amber column glyphs are not accepted member sizes.
  • Municipal acceptance of the zero-right setback and external WC position.
  • Final stair/guard/waist-slab design, finished headroom certification and vehicle turning/gate operation.
  • Final MEP sizing and details: drainage stacks and vents are shown for coordination; electrical protection/cables, fan sizing, final inverts and manufacturer selections are not designed.
  • A new occupied top-floor layout is excluded. Roof access is now modeled with 18 risers, a real roof void, a rear exit onto the terrace and 2400 mm enclosure clearance; structural supporting details remain unissued.
