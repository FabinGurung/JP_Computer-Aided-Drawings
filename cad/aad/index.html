Your first wall · four steps

  1. The example opens on Drawing floor with Draw connected wall enabled.
  2. Drag from start to end with your finger/mouse, or tap/click two points on the 2D plan.
  3. Inspect the new wall in 3D. Choose Draw connected wall again to add another; choose a wall and Host door/window to add an opening.
  4. Use Save working JSON to download your draft or Import working draft to restore it.

Practice workspace. This is fictional 8 × 6 m example geometry. Drawings are saved locally in this browser or downloaded as JSON; they are not uploaded to Drive, committed to GitHub, or approved for construction.
